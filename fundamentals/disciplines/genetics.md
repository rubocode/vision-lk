## Genetics

> A clone of Einstein wouldn't be stupid, but he wouldn't necessarily be any genius, either.  
> **James D. Watson**

Genetics is **what we are made of**.  It is the study of the ramifications of the unique DNA structure each individual receives upon conception.  Intelligence and capabilities depend quite a bit on the influence of the DNA.

Capabilities belong to three high level categories — **talents**, **skills** and **wisdom**.  All three can be applied to a task.  Talents are inborn and so is the ability learn skills, assimilate knowledge and develop wisdom.

Raw intelligence does not improve over a lifetime, but wisdom — _the gathered and integrated knowledge_ — can and does increase.  The ability to learn is a function of intelligence and wisdom.  They inform each other.

Genetics inform the development of personality — the way the brain perceives and judges.  This plays a significant role in personal development over a lifetime and optimal career strategies among other things.

This has serious implications for talent management and governance.  The gene pool establishes the identity and the unique advantage of a nation.

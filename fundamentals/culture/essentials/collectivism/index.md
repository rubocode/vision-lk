## Collectivism

> Resistance is futile, you will be assimilated.  
> **The Borg**

Collectivism takes the view that society needs to be organized top-down and must be managed — often controlled — from the top.

Liberation is to be facilitated by human organization of society.  Often material, sometimes spiritual.

The collective offers management of worldly affairs for its members.  Optionally, spiritual guidance.

The focus is on managing supply and demand for goods and services.

This is a _top-down_ approach.

The goal is power.

## Liberation

Obviously, we don’t have it all great.  There are trials and sorrows.  We wonder if there is a way out.  People have wondered for thousands of years.

### Spiritual Liberation

Is there a higher level to existence.  Something above the fray.  Something noble.

There are many who claim to have liberated themselves.  Some even taught how to do it yourself.

However, it is clear that you know you’re liberated only after you’re liberated.  Until then, you just trust and live by someone else’s prescription.  Or you could just wing it on your own.

The search for the truth and liberation has its own dynamic.  We sometimes call this the journey in the wilderness.

### Material Liberation

Of course, one might decide that there is no such thing as liberation.  That it is rather inconvenient to search for.  These people look for liberation via material possession and power.

### Victory

The true prize is for the courageous, the brave and the faithful.

## Peace

> Poverty is the parent of crime and revolution.  
> **Aristotle**

We have received an overdose of protests, conflict and revolts in the last half century.  Poverty is the injustice that is the root cause.

If we aim for peace, we must eliminate poverty and also provide hope for everyone.  The meritocracy is a key piece of the puzzle.

We also need to remain a peaceful country internationally and our foreign policy needs to reflect that.  We need to stand up for what is right, at least on a case by case basis.  Nonalignment is a timid cop out.

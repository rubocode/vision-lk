## Peace

> Poverty is the parent of crime and revolution.  
> **Aristotle**

We have received an overdose of protests, conflict and revolts in the last half century.  Poverty is the injustice that is the root cause.  If we aim for peace, we must eliminate poverty and also provide hope for everyone.  The meritocracy is a key piece of the puzzle.

> If the misery of the poor be caused not by the laws of nature, but by our institutions, great is our sin.  
> **Charles Darwin**

Our unique meritocracy must be institutionalized.  Justice and fair play will lead to a strong common platform.  Peaceful collaboration will to rapid progress.  If we know where we are going as a country, we can move away from the mentality of blowing out the other person’s candle to make our candle look good and working on better goals that will pay off inside the meritocracy.

We also need to remain a peaceful country internationally and our foreign policy needs to reflect that.  We need to stand up for what is right, at least on a case by case basis.  Nonalignment is a timid cop out.

> To be prepared for war is one of the most effective means of preserving peace.  
> **George Washington**

If we are to deal peacefully and intelligently in international affairs, we need to understand the rest of the world.  This means creating global citizens who have depth and breadth.  Our education must be globalist.

We need the world to value a Sri Lanka in any given situation.  We should have that unique brand that distinguishes us internationally.  And what better brand to have than **talented**, **intelligent** and **peaceful**.  That’s the way to arm ourselves for peace.

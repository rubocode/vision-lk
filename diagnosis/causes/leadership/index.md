## Leadership

> The supreme quality of leadership is integrity.  
> **Dwight Eisenhower**

It would have been a whole lot easier for the leadership to maintain integrity if only they knew the purpose of their existence.

If only they realized that their purpose is to govern the country towards liberty, wisdom and prosperity.

Currently, most of them think that politics is there for them to become rich and famous.

They may get rich, but history will not treat them kindly.

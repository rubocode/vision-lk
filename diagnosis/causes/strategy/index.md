## Strategy

> Instead of trying to be popular, try to be excellent.  
> **Ruchira Bomiriya**

If no leader before you figured it out, it is your first priority to figure it out.  Else, you’d be just like them.  People who thought they were leaders, but weren’t.

The ultimate objective for the leader is to figure out what is good for the country.  Wasting time trying to figure out how to gain and maintain power takes away so much time and energy from figuring out what’s good for the people.

If you figure it out, people will get on your ship.  If they don’t, you really didn’t figure it out just right.  You don’t deserve to be the leader.

Have faith that you can figure it out and tat you can do it.  People are just waiting to hear the good news!

## Management

The resource management of the country needs to be on a stable foundation.

Is it going to be a distributed structure with minimal government and a flourishing private sector?

Or is it going to be centrally controlled structure with state owned enterprises?

Did we ever make up our minds? Aren’t we just flip-flopping because we are too small minded to think big or long term?

Not very stable now, for sure!

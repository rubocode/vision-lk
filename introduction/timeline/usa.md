## USA

In 1990, I was awarded a scholarship by Stevens Institute of Technology in the USA.  I had obtained three perfect scores in my achievement tests in Chemistry, Physics and Mathematics.

All the dialogue in Sri Lanka in the early ‘80s was focused on **development (සංවර්ධනය)**.  It was even the larger portion in Social Studies text books in schools.  Here, I had landed in the most developed country in the world.  I would soon find out what a developed country looked like.  What is the difference?  How did they do it?

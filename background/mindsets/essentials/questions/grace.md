## Grace

Grace is when you are relieved of bad consequences of your actions.

If there is perfect Karma, you have to suffer all your mistakes.

If you can be pardoned for your bad decisions, there is room for improvement.

And, improvement is good.

Therefore, grace is good &mdash; if we can somehow find it.

That also means, there is something outside of Karma.

Or, is it the case that grace also needs to be requested.

That would be an action, wouldn't it?

With consequences!
